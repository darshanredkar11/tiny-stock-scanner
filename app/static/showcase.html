How to read this: Account labels are anonymized; no email addresses or user IDs are exposed. These are back-calculated estimates from recorded alerts and price history, not verified customer profits or executed trades. The purchase price and quantity are the values currently configured on the stock record, which may differ from their values when the alert fired. Results exclude brokerage, taxes, spread, slippage, partial fills and data gaps. A missing buy-back observation does not mean the price never reached the target; the feed may have missed it.

Market data uses a public Yahoo Finance feed and may be delayed, incomplete or rate-limited. The scanner is alert-only; it does not place orders.
